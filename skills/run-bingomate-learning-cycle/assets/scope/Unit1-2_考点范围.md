**教材范围：p.1 – p.8**（Section A: p.1–4；Section B: p.5–7；Self Check: p.8）

| 功能 Function | Talk about how to study（谈论学习方式） |
| 语法结构 Structure | Verb + `by` with gerund（by + 动名词表方式） |
| 复现语言 Recycling | be interested in, be good at, in common, get bored, find out, learn from, fall in love with, because of；How 引导的特殊疑问句 |

## 二、核心句型（Target Language）

### 1. Section A 1a–1c（p.1）
- `How do you study for a test?` — `I study by working with a group.`
- 学习方式表达（1a）：by working with friends / by making word cards / by reading the textbook / by listening to tapes / by asking the teacher for help

### 2. Section A 2a–2c（p.2）
- `Have you ever studied with a group?` — `Yes, I have. I've learned a lot that way.`
- `Do you have conversations with friends in English?`
- `What about listening to tapes? / What about reading aloud to practice pronunciation?`

### 3. Grammar Focus（p.4）—— 本单元语法核心表格
| 问句 | 答句 |
| --- | --- |
| How do you learn English? | I learn **by studying** with a group. |
| Do you learn English **by reading aloud**? | Yes, I do. It helps my pronunciation. |
| How can I read faster? | You can read faster **by reading word groups**. |
| How can I improve my pronunciation? | One way is **by listening to tapes**. |

### 4. Section B 1e（p.5）建议表达
- `I don't have a partner to practice English with.` — `Maybe you should join an English club.`

### 5. Section B 3b 写作句式（p.7）
- There are three good ways to ... / I think you should ... / If you do this, you will ...
- It is also a good idea to ... because ... / You could try to improve your English by ... / This will help you to ...

## 三、语法要点

### 1. by + 动名词（doing）表示"通过……方式"（p.1、p.4）
- 结构：**do sth. by doing sth.**
- 提问：`How do/can you ...?` → 答语常用 `By doing ...`
- 4a 配对练习中的高频搭配（p.4）：
  - practice speaking → by having conversations with friends
  - learn new words → by making word cards
  - improve writing → by writing e-mails to pen pals
  - practice listening → by watching English programs
  - improve pronunciation → by listening to a tape and repeating out loud
  - learn grammar → by taking notes, doing exercises and reading a lot
- 4b 拓展：用 math, physics, chemistry, Chinese, history, geography 等学科造 "do sth. by doing" 句。

### 2. by 表示"（一个）接着（一个）"（注释 p.113）
- word by word（一字一字地）、one by one、little by little、year by year、step by step
- 相关概念：**word group（意群）**——阅读时按意群读可提高速度与理解。

### 3. so ... that ... 结果状语从句（p.3 课文）
- `The teacher spoke so quickly that I did not understand her most of the time.`

### 4. "the + 比较级, the + 比较级"（p.2）
- `The more you read, the faster you'll be.`（读得越多，速度越快）

### 5. 主语从句 whether or not ...（注释 p.114）
- `But whether or not you can do this well depends on your learning habits.`
- `whether or not` ≈ `whether`，可拆开写：`whether ... or not`，用于主语从句、宾语从句。

### 6. some 与 any 的用法辨析（注释 p.114）
- 否定句中用 some 表示"其中一些"（并非全部）：`I can't pronounce some of the words.`
- 若用 any 则表示"所有的、任何的"，语义改变。
- 请求、建议、反问的疑问句中也常用 some：`Can I have some of your cake?`

### 7. 介词后动词用 -ing 形式（注释 p.115）
- `Knowledge comes from questioning.`（question 作动词，意为"质疑；质问"）

### 8. it 作形式主语 / find it + adj. + to do
- `Paul finds it difficult to learn English.`（p.5）
- `It is not enough to just study hard.`（p.6）

# Unit 2 I think that mooncakes are delicious!

**教材范围：p.9 – p.16**（Section A: p.9–12；Section B: p.13–15；Self Check: p.16）

## 一、单元目标（目录 p.II–III）

| 项目 | 内容 |
| --- | --- |
| 话题 Topic | Festivals（节日） |
| 功能 Function | Give a personal reaction（表达个人感受与看法） |
| 语法结构 Structure | Objective clauses with **that, if, whether**；**Exclamatory statements**（感叹句） |
| 复现语言 Recycling | fantastic, crowded, delicious, fun, traditional, pretty, beautiful |

## 二、核心句型（Target Language）

### 1. 表达观点与推测（p.9–p.10）
- `I think that they're fun to watch.`
- `I know that the Water Festival is really fun.`
- `I wonder if/whether they'll have the races again next year.`
- `I believe that April is the hottest month of the year there.`
- `I guess the food was delicious, right?`

### 2. Grammar Focus（p.12）—— 双栏对照
| 宾语从句 | 感叹句 |
| --- | --- |
| I know **that** the Water Festival is really fun. | **What** fun the Water Festival is! |
| I wonder **if** they'll have the races again next year. | **How** fantastic the dragon boat teams were! |
| I wonder **whether** June is a good time to visit Hong Kong. | **How** pretty the dragon boats were! |
| I believe **that** April is the hottest month in Thailand. | **How** delicious the food is in Hong Kong! |

### 3. Section B 1d 讨论句式（p.13）
- `What else do you know about the Lantern Festival?`
- `It falls on the 15th day of the first lunar month.`
- `I think it's great fun to solve the riddles.`

### 4. Section B 3b 写作句式（p.16 前，p.16 上方框）
- My favorite Chinese festival is ... / It is celebrated in/on ... / During this festival, people ... / It's my favorite festival because ... / It makes me feel ...
- **写作提纲**：先介绍节日名称与时间 → 再写人们的活动与食物 → 最后说明喜爱原因与感受。

## 三、语法要点

### 1. 宾语从句（Objective Clauses）—— 核心考点（p.12；语法附录 p.152）
- **定义**：复合句中由一个句子充当宾语。
- **引导词**：
  - **that**：引导陈述句内容，口语或非正式文体中常省略。`I think (that) Halloween is a fun festival.`
  - **if / whether**："是否"，口语中常用 if。`I wonder if/whether they'll have the races again next year.`
  - **wh- 疑问词**（who, what, which, when, where, how, why）：见 Unit 3。
- **三大注意点**：① 从句用**陈述语序**；② 时态遵循主从时态呼应；③ if/whether 与 that 不可混用。
- **练习出处**：4a 用所给词造句（p.12）、4b 划出短文中的宾语从句（p.12）。

### 2. 感叹句（Exclamatory Statements）（p.12、p.16）
| 结构 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **What** + (a/an) + adj. + n. (+ 主语 + 谓语)! | What fun the Water Festival is! / What a nice treat! |
| **How** + adj./adv. + 主语 + 谓语! | How fantastic the dragon boat teams were! / How delicious the food is! |
- Self Check 2（p.16）将陈述句改写为感叹句，是本单元固定题型。

### 3. 表语从句（注释 p.116）
- `One very important custom **is that** people will all try to return home ...`
- 表语从句位于系动词之后，陈述语序；陈述内容用 that，疑问内容用 whether 或特殊疑问词引导。
- 拓展例：`The only question is whether the situation can be improved.` / `That's why she's so angry.`

### 4. 现在完成进行时（p.11 课文）
- `Chinese people **have been celebrating** the Mid-Autumn Festival ... for centuries.`

### 5. so ... that ... 与复合不定代词 whoever（p.11）
- `Hou Yi was so sad that he called out her name to the moon every night.`
- `Whoever took this could live forever.`（whoever = anyone who，引导主语从句）

### 6. when 引导的定语从句（p.14）
- `New Year is a wonderful time **when** family and friends come together ...`
