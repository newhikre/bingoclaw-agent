## 怎么用这份边界

词汇分三类，出题前对照：

| 类别 | 处置 |
| --- | --- |
| **本单元考点词**（下面列出的） | 鼓励使用，考点应当落在这些词与结构上 |
| **通用基础词**（初中前两年的常用词，如 go / school / bus / friend / every day） | 允许使用，用来搭句子 |
| **超纲词与超纲结构**（本文件没有，且属于 Unit 3 及以后或高中内容） | **禁止**。最典型的越界是 wh- 疑问词引导的宾语从句（Unit 3）与被动语态 |

判断标准是**考点必须落在本文件内**。用一个通用基础词搭句子没问题，用一条本文件没有的语法去考学生就是越界。

拿不准某个词算不算通用基础词时，换一个本文件里出现过的词——变式题的价值在考点，不在词汇新鲜度。

# Unit 1 · How can we become good learners?

话题：Learning how to learn　功能：谈论学习方式

## 语法结构

1. **`by` + 动名词表示"通过……的方式"** —— 本单元核心。结构 `do sth. by doing sth.`；提问 `How do/can you ...?`，答语 `By doing ...`
   - 高频搭配：practice speaking by having conversations with friends / learn new words by making word cards / improve writing by writing e-mails to pen pals / practice listening by watching English programs / improve pronunciation by listening to a tape and repeating out loud / learn grammar by taking notes, doing exercises and reading a lot
2. **`by` 表示"一个接着一个"** —— word by word、one by one、little by little、year by year、step by step
3. **`so ... that ...` 结果状语从句** —— The teacher spoke so quickly that I did not understand her.
4. **`the + 比较级, the + 比较级`** —— The more you read, the faster you'll be.
5. **`whether or not ...` 主语从句** —— whether or not 可拆写为 whether ... or not
6. **否定句中 some 与 any 的辨析** —— 否定句用 some 表示"其中一些"；用 any 表示"所有的、任何的"，语义改变。请求、建议、反问的疑问句中也常用 some
7. **介词后动词用 -ing 形式** —— Knowledge comes from questioning.
8. **`it` 作形式主语 / `find it + adj. + to do`** —— Paul finds it difficult to learn English. / It is not enough to just study hard.

## 核心句型

- How do you study for a test? — I study by working with a group.
- Have you ever studied with a group? — Yes, I have. I've learned a lot that way.
- Do you have conversations with friends in English?
- What about listening to tapes? / What about reading aloud to practice pronunciation?
- How can I read faster? — You can read faster by reading word groups.
- I don't have a partner to practice English with. — Maybe you should join an English club.

写作句式：There are three good ways to ... / I think you should ... / If you do this, you will ... / It is also a good idea to ... because ... / You could try to improve your English by ... / This will help you to ...

## 词汇

textbook, conversation, aloud, pronunciation, sentence, patient, secret, expression, discover, grammar, repeat, note, pal, physics, chemistry, memorize, pattern, novel, pronounce, increase, speed, review, partner, born, ability, create, brain, active, attention, connect, overnight, knowledge, lifelong, wisely

人名地名：Annie、Alexander Graham Bell

## 短语与习语

study for a test, make word cards, read aloud, word by word, word groups, be patient, fall in love with, look ... up (in a dictionary), take notes, keep a diary, make mistakes, in common, get bored, be good at, pay attention to, be born with, connect ... with, mind map, stressed out, worry about, be interested in, find out, learn from, because of

谚语与习语：a piece of cake（小菜一碟）、It serves you right.（你活该）、It takes time.（得慢慢来）、Use it or lose it.（用进废退）、Practice makes perfect.（熟能生巧）

# Unit 2 · I think that mooncakes are delicious!

话题：Festivals　功能：表达个人感受与看法

## 语法结构

1. **宾语从句（that / if / whether）** —— 本单元核心
   - `that` 引导陈述内容，口语中常省略：I think (that) Halloween is a fun festival.
   - `if / whether` 表"是否"：I wonder if/whether they'll have the races again next year.
   - 三条注意点：从句用**陈述语序**；主从时态呼应；if/whether 与 that 不可混用
   - **wh- 疑问词引导的宾语从句属于 Unit 3，不在本边界内**
2. **感叹句**
   - `What` + (a/an) + adj. + n. (+ 主语 + 谓语)! —— What fun the Water Festival is! / What a nice treat!
   - `How` + adj./adv. + 主语 + 谓语! —— How fantastic the dragon boat teams were! / How delicious the food is!
3. **表语从句** —— One very important custom is that people will all try to return home. 陈述内容用 that，疑问内容用 whether 或特殊疑问词
4. **现在完成进行时** —— Chinese people have been celebrating the Mid-Autumn Festival for centuries.
5. **`so ... that ...` 与 `whoever` 引导主语从句** —— Hou Yi was so sad that he called out her name. / Whoever took this could live forever.（whoever = anyone who）
6. **`when` 引导的定语从句** —— New Year is a wonderful time when family and friends come together.

## 核心句型

- I think that they're fun to watch.
- I know that the Water Festival is really fun.
- I wonder if/whether they'll have the races again next year.
- I believe that April is the hottest month of the year there.
- I guess the food was delicious, right?
- What else do you know about the Lantern Festival?
- It falls on the 15th day of the first lunar month.
- I think it's great fun to solve the riddles.

写作句式：My favorite Chinese festival is ... / It is celebrated in/on ... / During this festival, people ... / It's my favorite festival because ... / It makes me feel ...

## 词汇

复现形容词：fantastic, crowded, delicious, fun, traditional, pretty, beautiful

地名：Macao、Chiang Mai

## 明确的禁区

出题时踩到这些一律作废重来：

- **wh- 疑问词引导的宾语从句**（who / what / which / when / where / how / why 引导）—— Unit 3 内容
- **被动语态**作为考点 —— 本两单元不考
- 本文件之外的语法结构，即使初中范围内也不用——测的应当是本次锚点，不是词汇量或课外积累
- 需要额外背景知识的题材（外国历史、专业领域、成人生活场景）
- 本文件没有、也不属于初中前两年常用词的生词
